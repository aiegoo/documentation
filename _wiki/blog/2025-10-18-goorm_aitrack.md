### **Key Features of This Roadmap**

1. **Progressive Difficulty**: Projects are structured with increasing complexity from basic text generation to advanced conversational AI systems.

2. **Practical Focus**: Each mission includes hands-on implementation with specific technologies and frameworks.

3. **Team Collaboration**: Team projects emphasize collaborative development and real-world application scenarios.

4. **Individual Growth**: Personal missions allow for specialized skill development in specific AI domains.

5. **Technology Stack**: Covers modern AI technologies including:
   * **Deep Learning**: PyTorch, Keras, TensorFlow
   * **NLP**: BERT, GPT-2, BART, NLTK
   * **Computer Vision**: OpenCV, GANs, Autoencoders
   * **Data Science**: SHAP, t-SNE, Bayesian Optimization
   * **Development Tools**: Figma, APIs, Graph Neural Networks

### **Implementation Strategy**

1. **Assessment**: Evaluate current skill level and choose appropriate starting point
2. **Track Selection**: Select specialized track based on career goals and interests
3. **Progressive Learning**: Complete individual missions to build foundational skills
4. **Team Collaboration**: Participate in team projects for practical experience
5. **Continuous Improvement**: Apply optimization and analysis techniques to ongoing projects
